Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about buying vehicles on VeloMart

General Questions

How do I buy a vehicle?

Browse our vehicle listings, select the one you want, add it to your cart, and proceed to checkout. Fill in your shipping and payment information, then place your order. Our team will contact you to finalize the details.

Can I make a payment online?

Yes! We accept multiple online payment methods including GCash, bank transfer, and credit/debit cards. You can also choose Cash on Delivery if you prefer to pay when you receive the vehicle.

Is it safe to purchase from VeloMart?

Absolutely! All our vehicles are verified and inspected. We ensure secure transactions and provide buyer protection. Your personal and payment information is encrypted and secure.

Payment & Pricing

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept GCash, bank transfer (online banking), credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, JCB), and Cash on Delivery (COD).

Are the prices negotiable?

Our listed prices are competitive and fair. However, you can contact us through the contact form or call our customer service to discuss pricing for bulk purchases or special circumstances.

Do you offer financing options?

We're currently working on financing partnerships. Please contact our sales team for information about available financing options and requirements.

Shipping & Delivery

How long does delivery take?

Delivery time varies depending on your location and vehicle availability. Typically, vehicles are delivered within 7-14 business days after order confirmation. We'll contact you to schedule a convenient delivery time.

Is delivery free?

Yes, we offer free delivery for all vehicle purchases within Metro Manila and surrounding areas. Delivery fees may apply for remote locations.

Can I pick up the vehicle myself?

Yes! You can arrange to pick up the vehicle from our location. Please coordinate with our team after placing your order to schedule a pickup time.

Vehicle Information

Are all vehicles inspected?

Yes, all vehicles undergo thorough inspection before being listed on our platform. We verify their condition, documentation, and history to ensure quality.

Can I return a vehicle if I'm not satisfied?

We encourage customers to thoroughly inspect vehicles before purchase. Return policies vary by situation. Please contact us within 48 hours of delivery if you have concerns, and we'll work with you to find a solution.

Do vehicles come with warranty?

Warranty coverage depends on the vehicle type and condition. Brand new vehicles come with manufacturer warranties. Used vehicles may have limited warranties. Check individual vehicle listings for specific warranty information.

Still have questions?

Can't find the answer you're looking for? Our team is here to help.
